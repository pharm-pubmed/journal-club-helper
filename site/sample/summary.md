## Key results

- CER (assumed control event rate): 29.8%
- EER (experimental event rate): 24.7%
- ARR (absolute risk reduction): 5.1 percentage points (95% CI 1.8 to 8.0)
- RRR (relative risk reduction): 17.0%
- NNT 20 (95% CI 13–56)

Notes on these numbers:

- This absolute effect is estimated from an assumed control event rate of 29.8%, so it only applies to patients whose untreated risk is close to that figure.
