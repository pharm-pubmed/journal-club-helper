A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Does it summarize papers for me?

It provides a structured appraisal workspace: study design, PICO, effect calculations, and a checklist. You confirm the inputs, assess the evidence, and write your own bottom line. Always check the full text.

Which study designs are supported?

Randomized controlled trials, cohort studies, case-control studies, cross-sectional studies, diagnostic studies, and systematic reviews / meta-analyses have their own checklists. An unclear design has a general checklist to help you get started.

Can I use it without an account?

Yes. The core workflow runs locally in Chrome or Edge with no account. Your appraisals are saved per article in the browser. You can copy summaries as Markdown or plain text, or download them.

Is it a clinical decision-support tool?

It is an educational aid for critical appraisal and journal club preparation. It gives no clinical advice or treatment recommendations. Verify the results and your interpretation against the full text.
