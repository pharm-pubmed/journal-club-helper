Journal Club Helper Privacy

Critical appraisal, next to the abstract you are reading

A side panel for PubMed that sets up your journal club appraisal: the study design, PICO, the absolute effect with the NNT, and a checklist for the design. It does not summarize the paper and it does not tell you what to conclude. You make every judgement.

Add to Chrome, free Chrome and Edge. No account, no analytics.

What it does

The absolute effect, not just the relative one

Pick a result from the abstract and see the absolute risk reduction, the relative risk reduction and the number needed to treat, with a 95% confidence interval when the numbers allow one. You confirm every number before it is used.

Risk of bias, one question at a time

A checklist written for each study design. Every question says what to look for, why it matters, and whether the abstract can settle it or you need the full text.

A one-page summary for journal club

Citation, clinical question, design, key results with the absolute effect, validity and your own bottom line. Copy it as Markdown or plain text, or download it.

Who it is for

  • Medical and pharmacy residents preparing journal club.
  • Medical, pharmacy and nursing students learning evidence-based medicine.
  • Anyone who teaches critical appraisal and wants a shared structure.

Your notes stay on your device

There is no account, no server and no analytics. Appraisals are saved in your browser, one per article, and you can export or delete them at any time. The extension's only network request is made when you ask for it: if a page cannot be read, it can fetch the same record from NCBI, sending the article's PubMed ID and nothing you wrote.
