# Japanese glossary (#82)

The Japanese UI, checklists and summary use these terms. Keep them consistent: change a term here first, then everywhere it appears. EBM terms follow common Japanese usage in pharmacy and medical education. Where two are common, the first is used, and the other may appear once in parentheses on first use.

**Status:** draft, not yet reviewed by the owner. The checklist and caveat terms are reviewed when their translation lands.
